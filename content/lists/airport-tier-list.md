## What makes a good airport?
In my opinion, good airports:
* Don't use buses
* Are easy to navigate
* Let you watch the planes take off and land from a nice little lounge or seating area.

[See data on all my flights in the last 10+ years here!](https://my.flightmemory.com/GordyNumberOne)

## S Tier Airports
These airports are a destination in and of themselves. These airports do great airport things, like:
* Walk or rail to gate, no buses
* Jet-bridges for all the planes, all the time
* Easy-to-read signage for efficient navigation
* Reasonably priced food & drinks

## A Tier Airports
To be A tier is not bad! These airports are great to transit through and are unlikely to give you issues. They all have:
* Efficient transit to gates, which may include busses
* Clean facilities, if not the most modern

## B Tier Airports
These are airports that are too small to be A tier or S tier, yet are still great in their own way. I particularly love small airports like KK or Charleston because you can go from the plane to your car in less than 10 minutes!

### Kota Kinabalu
Did you know the whole airport is shaped like an airfoil? That kind of architectural determinism is admirable. And on clear days, you can see the Mountain from the arrivals lounge.

### Charleston
This is the best small airport I've flown through in the US. It's got everything you need in a tiny package:
* Planes ✅
* Cute K-9 "officers"  ✅
* Chick-fil-a ✅
* Clean restrooms  ✅

### Kuala Lumpur International Airport, Terminal 2

```img
src="https://media.gordyclark.com/20260921-113610-xv72-test.jpg"
align="center"
alt="My favorite Ayam Penyet in KLIA2 Food court"
caption="My favorite Ayam Penyet in KLIA2 Food court"
```
Home of my favorite Ayam Penyet place! To get there:

#### From the KLIA Express
Go out and turn right, heading towards the wall with the **single working public restroom**. Put your right hand on the wall and walk forward until you find the food court. In the far back left, you'll see a stall with the sign **AYAM PENYET** -- go there and order the ayam penyet!

#### From arrivals
Go out and turn left, and go all the way down to the clinic and restrooms. Use the restrooms to wash your hands -- it is a good habit, and those sinks are better than the ones in the food court. Then, with your left hand on the wall, walk forward and down the ramp. It'll take you into the food court, turn left, and you should see the **AYAM PENYET** sign. Go towards the light and get your heavenly chicken.

## C Tier Airports

### Langkawi
Tiny little airport, no jet bridges, and more taxi/tour/scam spam than airport personnel.

It's got a franchise branch of US customs and border patrol. The agents there are unhappy that they can't use their guns indiscriminately (it's still Canada, after all), so they make up for it by being absolute narcissistic little bitches about every last thing.

Another tiny airport that doesn't impede you from starting your vacation, but doesn't help start it off in the best way, either.
